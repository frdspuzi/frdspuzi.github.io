# Personal Website

The site's research discovery feature helps its owner stay informed about new findings. This glossary records the terms settled during its design interview.

## Language

**Research discovery**:
Keeping up with new research findings and discoveries relevant to the site owner's interests.

**Research brief**:
A short, source-grounded summary of a research paper paired with a practical use case and an important limitation.

**Research relevance**:
A finding's connection to the owner's selected research interests: technology, Islamic Studies and finance, and career/productivity. Relevant theoretical work can also merit an accessible brief.

**Peer-reviewed paper**:
A research paper assessed by subject specialists through a journal or conference's formal review process. This scrutiny does not itself establish independent reproduction of the findings.

**Research topic**:
A subject label used to group and filter research briefs. The three topics are Tech, Islamic Studies & Finance, and Career & Productivity.

**Islamic Studies & Finance**:
Academic research about Islam, Muslim life, and Islamic finance within the discovery feed. Religious guidance, such as scholarly explanations of scripture or rulings, is outside this topic's scope for this feature.
