# Personal Website

The site's research discovery feature helps its owner stay informed about new findings. This glossary records the terms settled during its design interview.

## Language

**Research discovery**:
Keeping up with new research findings and discoveries relevant to the site owner's interests.

**Research brief**:
A short, source-grounded card for one research paper with four parts: the problem it addresses, the fix the authors built or found, why it matters, and a caveat.

**Research relevance**:
A finding's connection to the owner's selected research interests: technology, Islamic Studies and finance, and career/productivity. Relevant theoretical work can also merit an accessible brief.

**Peer-reviewed paper**:
A research paper assessed by subject specialists through a journal or conference's formal review process. This scrutiny does not itself establish independent reproduction of the findings.

**Research topic**:
A subject label used to group and filter research briefs. The three topics are Tech, Islamic Studies & Finance, and Career & Productivity.

**Bookshelf**:
The public collection of books the site owner has read or is reading, each shown by its cover.

**Reading notes**:
The content shown for one book, written by the owner in their own words: a short overall take first, followed by their insights in reading order. It is never a reproduction or summary of the book's own content, which the owner has no right to share.

**Insight**:
The owner's own takeaway from one passage of a book, tagged with that passage's page range. It may quote at most one attributed sentence from the book and may carry the owner's own Islamic reflection. In this feature, "page" means only the book's own printed pages, never a unit of the reading notes.
_Avoid_: Note, summary

**TLDR**:
An AI-generated summary of one passage of a book, kept as a private reading aid for writing insights. TLDRs are never published and never feed trivia questions.

**Trivia question**:
A multiple-choice question on the site's quiz board, with a short explanation of the answer. Each one comes from a single source, either one of the owner's Medium articles or one of their insights, and must be answerable from that source alone.

**Currently reading**:
A book on the bookshelf that the owner hasn't finished. Its reading notes stop where the owner has reached and grow as reading continues.

**Islamic Studies & Finance**:
Academic research about Islam, Muslim life, and Islamic finance within the discovery feed. Religious guidance, such as scholarly explanations of scripture or rulings, is outside this topic's scope for this feature.
